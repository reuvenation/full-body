Delete your account and data

FullBody app (formerly “Full Body Tracker”, com.reuvenation.fullbody) · Last updated: October 1, 2026 · Русская версия

You can delete your account and all data linked to it at any time. There are two ways — inside the app, or by email request.

Option 1. In the app (fastest)

  1. Open the app or the website fullbody-tracker.ru and sign in.
  2. Tap the profile button in the top-right corner.
  3. Choose “Delete account” and confirm.

Workouts, records and measurements are deleted immediately and cannot be recovered.

Option 2. By email request

Write from the address your account is registered with, so we can verify the request is yours.

Email reuvenations@gmail.com

Requests are processed within 10 business days.

What exactly is deleted

DataWhat happens
Workout history: exercises, sets, weight, reps, recordsDeleted completely and permanently
Body metrics: body weight, body-fat percentage, measurementsDeleted completely and permanently
Account: email, name, password (hash), body-figure typeDeleted completely and permanently
Local copy of your data on the deviceErased when you delete the account, sign out, or uninstall the app

Nothing is kept or archived after deletion: we keep no backups containing your workouts — the additional retention period is 0 days.

If you have no account

You can use the app without an account — then your data lives only on your device. To erase it, uninstall the app or clear the app’s data in Android settings (for the website, clear the site data in your browser).
